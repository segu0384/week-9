Stuff about swans

Did you know that about 20%-25% of black swans are homosexual? They will even steal the eggs of a female to raise them for themselves. They will mate for life.

Swans are graceful waterbirds known for their long necks and powerful wings. They often live on lakes and rivers, where they feed on aquatic plants.
